# Market Basket Analysis

**Status:** In progress
**Updated:** 2026-11-04

## Goal
Extend the Sales semantic model so Ana can find products bought together and evaluate cross-sell, bundles and shelf placement. Capture the requirements from issue #39 and the 2026-11-04 working session for approval before implementation.

## Scope
- Change: Add dynamic MBA measures, Product B selection, Product/Subcategory/Category exploration, minimum Support and Lift parameters, and MBA pages in the existing sales-overview report.
- Keep unchanged: Existing sales and customer measures, existing tests, unrelated report pages, HR artifacts, source credentials and the existing Sales refresh schedule. No separate dataset or changes under scripts/.

## Approach
The source of truth is [sales.SemanticModel](../../src/sales.SemanticModel). [Sales.tmdl](../../src/sales.SemanticModel/definition/tables/Sales.tmdl) has line-level Order Number, Line Number, ProductKey, CustomerKey, StoreKey, Order Date and Quantity. [Product.tmdl](../../src/sales.SemanticModel/definition/tables/Product.tmdl) has Product, Subcategory and Category. Calendar, Customer and Store actively filter Sales. The developer approved using available data in PR comment 6631527385: use Store Country/State for geography, defer Customer Segment and Sales Channel until mappings arrive, and add no new RLS. Do not invent missing attributes.

Use Sales[Order Number] as the approved basket key, matching the issue's Sales Order Number definition. Validate its uniqueness across customers, dates and stores. Quantity > 0 is the approved full line-eligibility and return-exclusion rule for this version; exclude blank order numbers. Deduplicate repeated products within each basket. Add a disconnected Product (Basket) copy of the Product attributes for Product B; use TREATAS to apply B keys when building its order set. No relationship is needed with this approach. Build separate qualifying order sets for A and B, removing A's Product filter when evaluating B, then intersect order numbers. Do not try to find A and B on the same sales line.

Put explicit, described UPPERCASE measures in Sales under the Market Basket display folder: TOTAL ORDERS, ORDERS WITH A, ORDERS WITH B, ORDERS WITH A AND B, SUPPORT, CONFIDENCE, LIFT, AVG BASKET SIZE and AVG ITEMS PER ORDER. TOTAL ORDERS clears both product-role filters but preserves Date, Customer and Store context. Support is joint orders / total orders; Confidence is joint orders / A orders; Lift is Confidence / (B orders / total orders). AVG BASKET SIZE is distinct order/product combinations / total qualifying orders; AVG ITEMS PER ORDER is positive units / total qualifying orders. These two definitions were approved in comment 6631527385. Use safe division and return blank for undefined ratios. Suppress identical A/B members at the active grain and do not rank ambiguous multi-member totals as pairs. Retain the same qualifying-line and filter rules in shared basket logic, optionally a DAX UDF if it reduces duplication.

Expose matching Category > Subcategory > Product hierarchies for A and B. Add numeric what-if parameters for minimum Support (0 to 1 in steps of 0.001; default 0.005, displayed as 0.5%) and minimum Lift (0 to 10 in steps of 0.1; default 1.2). These are editable initial control ranges, not bounds on raw metrics. A separate eligibility measure hides pairs below either threshold without changing the raw metrics or their denominators. Add business descriptions identifying the A-to-B direction, denominator, grain and exclusions so Copilot and Q&A can interpret the metrics.

Extend [sales-overview.Report](../../src/sales-overview.Report), preserving its existing model binding. Pages: MBA overview with three KPI cards and the top 20 qualifying product pairs by Lift; Product drill with a searchable Product A slicer and an associated-product table showing counts, Support, Confidence and conditionally formatted Lift; Association matrix with Category A × Category B Lift; Pair trend with monthly Support and Lift for a selected pair; and an order-details drillthrough page showing the first 100 qualifying orders by order number. Carry both pair roles and current filters into drillthrough, with a back action and a clear sample label. Use the filter pane for Date, geography, pair selections and thresholds, with at most one slicer per page. Implementation of this page plan is approved; Ana's rendered-page review remains an acceptance check, not a claim of sign-off.

The order-details flow uses four hidden destinations, each binding only its matching pair fields: Product, Subcategory, Category and the observed overview pair. This avoids requiring Product fields on Category/Subcategory source visuals. Each destination groups by Order Number only, preserving a global 100-order sample under selected business filters rather than restarting the limit per date or store. The four visible MBA pages and original report pages are unchanged by this routing detail.

After approval, implement in src/, run local BPA on a Windows host using the existing [bpa.ps1](../../scripts/bpa/bpa.ps1), deploy with [deploy.py](../../scripts/deploy.py), refresh with [refresh.py](../../scripts/refresh.py) and run [test-data.py](../../scripts/test-data.py). Add MBA assertions to Tests.Run in [functions.tmdl](../../src/sales.SemanticModel/definition/functions.tmdl), preserving existing assertions. Preview the report against the development workspace model. Measure full-history visual execution before considering an Order-Product distinct table; add that optimization only if needed and preserve filter/security behavior. Update the existing Sales model/report documentation with approved definitions and validation evidence.

The first full-history Cartesian product-pair query timed out after 90 seconds. Use a disconnected calculated Basket Pair table containing only observed co-purchase candidate identities for the overview, with a dynamic PAIR RANK measure ordered by Lift and then A/B keys. This narrows the search space without storing static Support, Confidence or Lift. Candidate identities refresh inside Sales; all metrics and ranks still recalculate under the current business filters. Other pages retain the two product-role dimensions and their hierarchies.

## Acceptance checks
- Validate three real, named product pairs against independently calculated distinct qualifying order sets. Record pair keys, filter context, A count, B count, joint count, total orders and expected Support, Confidence and Lift. Do not claim fixtures are known until queried.
- Verify repeated lines count a product once per order; zero/negative quantities and confirmed returns are excluded; no-order and zero-denominator scopes are safe; identical Product, Subcategory and Category pairs are hidden. Reversing A/B preserves Support and Lift but recalculates directional Confidence.
- At each grain, compare unfiltered, monthly and combined Date/Customer/Store/Country/State scopes against the independent order-set calculation. Segment/Channel checks are deferred until mappings are supplied. B's prevalence and total orders must not inherit A's product filter. Threshold changes affect pair visibility and the top 20, not the underlying metric values.
- After approval of their definitions, validate both basket-average KPIs on orders with repeated product lines and multiple units. Validate parameter defaults, threshold boundaries and one Tests.Run assertion for every new measure; all existing assertions and baseline sales/customer results must remain unchanged.
- The model loads without errors; new measures have descriptions, formats and the Market Basket folder; BPA has no new violations. Test Copilot/Q&A wording against actual product names rather than assuming mountain bikes exist in this catalog.
- Render all new pages against the development model, verify Lift formatting, the top-20 limit, selected-pair monthly trends and drillthrough samples against joint order sets, and record Ana's sign-off. Existing pages still render and retain their filters and measures.
- Record full-history timings for each new visual with representative broad and selective filters, cold and warm runs, targeting under 3 seconds. Preserve existing security; no new RLS is approved for this version. Confirm scheduled Sales refresh updates Product B and MBA results without a second dataset or schedule.
